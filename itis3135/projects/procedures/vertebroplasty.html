Vertebroplasty

What is the procedure?

Vertebroplasty is a minimally invasive procedure that injects cement into a fractured vertebra to stabilize it and relieve pain. This procedure is used to treat painful vertebral compression fractures caused by osteoporosis, cancer, or trauma.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your vertebroplasty:

  • Tell your doctor about all medications, especially blood thinners
  • Fasting for 6-8 hours is usually required
  • Have imaging studies (X-ray, CT, or MRI) completed beforehand
  • Arrange for transportation home after the procedure
  • Wear a hospital gown and remove all metal items

After the procedure

Following your vertebroplasty:

  • Rest for the remainder of the day
  • You may resume normal activities gradually
  • Ice can be applied to the injection site for comfort
  • Take pain medication as needed
  • Avoid strenuous activity and heavy lifting for 1-2 weeks
  • Report any fever, severe pain, or neurological symptoms
  • Attend all follow-up appointments
