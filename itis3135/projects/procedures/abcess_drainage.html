Abcess Drainage

What is the procedure?

Abcess drainage is a minimally invasive procedure used to treat bacterial infections that have accumulated as pus in body cavities. The procedure involves accessing the abcess under imaging guidance and draining the infected fluid to relieve symptoms and resolve the infection.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your abcess drainage procedure:

  • Inform your doctor of any medications you are taking
  • Fast for 4-6 hours before the procedure if sedation will be used
  • Arrange for transportation home after the procedure
  • Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing
  • Remove any metal objects or jewelry

After the procedure

Following your abcess drainage:

  • Rest for the remainder of the day
  • Take prescribed antibiotics as directed
  • Monitor the drainage site for signs of infection
  • Avoid strenuous activities for 1-2 weeks
  • Attend all follow-up appointments
  • Contact your doctor if you experience fever, increased pain, or unusual discharge
