Gallbladder Drain Placement

What is the procedure?

Gallbladder drain placement is a minimally invasive procedure that inserts a catheter into the gallbladder to drain bile in cases of obstruction or infection. This procedure relieves pain and pressure while allowing the gallbladder to function or heal.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your gallbladder drain placement:

  • Discuss any medications with your doctor, especially anticoagulants
  • Fast for 6-8 hours prior to the procedure
  • Arrange for a ride home after the procedure
  • Wear comfortable, easy-to-remove clothing
  • Remove all metal items and jewelry

After the procedure

Following your gallbladder drain placement:

  • Rest for the remainder of the day
  • Keep the drain site clean and protected
  • Monitor drain output daily
  • Empty and clean the drain bag as directed
  • Avoid heavy lifting and strenuous activity
  • Return for follow-up imaging or drain adjustments as needed
