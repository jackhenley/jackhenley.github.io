Percutaneous Gastrostomy

What is the procedure?

Percutaneous gastrostomy (PEG tube placement) is a procedure that inserts a feeding tube directly into the stomach through the abdominal wall. This tube provides nutrition and medications for patients who cannot eat or swallow normally.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your percutaneous gastrostomy:

  • Notify your doctor of all medications
  • Fast for 6-8 hours before the procedure
  • Arrange for someone to drive you home
    • \n
  • Wear a hospital gown
  • Have baseline blood work completed

After the procedure

Following your percutaneous gastrostomy:

  • Rest for 24 hours after placement
  • Keep the tube site clean and dry
  • Do not eat or drink until cleared by your doctor
  • Start feedings gradually as instructed
  • Flush the tube with water before and after feedings
  • Watch for signs of infection (fever, redness, drainage)
  • Attend all follow-up appointments and dietary consultations
