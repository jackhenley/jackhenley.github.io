Biliary Drain Placement

What is the procedure?

Biliary drain placement is a minimally invasive procedure that inserts a catheter into the bile ducts to relieve obstruction and allow bile to drain. This procedure is used when the normal flow of bile is blocked due to tumors, strictures, or stones.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your biliary drain placement:

  • Notify your doctor of all medications, especially blood thinners
  • Fast for 6-8 hours before the procedure
  • Arrange for someone to drive you home
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Bring an ID and insurance card

After the procedure

Following your biliary drain placement:

  • Keep the drain site clean and dry
  • Monitor drain output and character
  • Take prescribed medications as directed
  • Avoid heavy lifting and strenuous activity for 2-3 weeks
  • Return for scheduled drain flushes or replacements
  • Report any fever, increased drainage, or abdominal pain
