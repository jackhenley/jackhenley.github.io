Nephrostomy Tube Placement

What is the procedure?

Nephrostomy tube placement is a procedure that inserts a catheter directly into the kidney to bypass obstructed or damaged ureters. The tube allows urine to drain from the kidney externally when normal urinary drainage is blocked.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your nephrostomy tube placement:

  • Inform your doctor of all current medications
  • Fasting for 4-6 hours may be required
  • Have baseline blood work and imaging completed
  • Arrange for a responsible adult to take you home
  • Wear a hospital gown with nothing underneath except underwear

After the procedure

Following your nephrostomy tube placement:

  • Rest and avoid strenuous activity for several days
  • Keep the tube site clean and dry
  • Monitor urine output and report any changes
  • Empty the collection bag regularly
  • Take pain medication and antibiotics as prescribed
  • Attend all follow-up appointments
  • Report fever, back pain, or bloody urine
