PICC Line Placement

What is the procedure?

PICC (Peripherally Inserted Central Catheter) line placement is a procedure that inserts a long, thin tube into a vein in the arm and advances it to the chest near the heart. PICC lines provide long-term access for medications, fluids, and blood draws.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your PICC line placement:

  • Inform your doctor about bleeding disorders or medications
  • No fasting is usually required
  • Wear loose, short-sleeved clothing for arm access
  • Remove jewelry from both arms
  • Arrange comfortable seating as the procedure takes 30-60 minutes

After the procedure

Following your PICC line placement:

  • Keep the line site clean and covered with sterile dressing
  • Avoid getting the line wet until directed by your doctor
  • Do not use the arm for blood pressure measurements or blood draws
  • Avoid heavy lifting with the catheter arm
  • Report any pain, swelling, or redness at the site
  • Follow care instructions for dressing changes and flushes
  • Keep all follow-up appointments for imaging confirmation
