Percutaneous Biopsy

What is the procedure?

Percutaneous biopsy is a minimally invasive procedure that collects tissue samples from organs or lesions using a needle guided by imaging (ultrasound, CT, or fluoroscopy). The samples are analyzed to diagnose conditions such as cancer, infection, or inflammation.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your percutaneous biopsy:

  • Tell your doctor about blood thinning medications
  • Fasting for 4-6 hours may be recommended
  • Arrange for transportation home
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Remove jewelry and metal accessories
  • Have recent imaging studies available for the radiologist

After the procedure

Following your percutaneous biopsy:

  • Rest for the remainder of the day
  • Apply ice to the biopsy site if needed for discomfort
  • Keep the biopsy site clean and dry
  • Avoid strenuous activity for 24-48 hours
  • Take pain relief medication as needed
  • Call your doctor if you experience bleeding, fever, or severe pain
  • Follow up for biopsy results within 1-2 weeks
