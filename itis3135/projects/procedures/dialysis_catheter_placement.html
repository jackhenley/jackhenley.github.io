Dialysis Catheter Placement

What is the procedure?

Dialysis catheter placement is a procedure that inserts a catheter into a large vein to provide vascular access for hemodialysis. This allows blood to be removed, filtered by a dialysis machine, and returned to the body in patients with kidney failure.

Preparing for the procedure

Before your dialysis catheter placement:

  • Inform your doctor about any bleeding disorders or medications
  • Fasting may be required depending on anesthesia plans
  • Have a blood clotting test performed if needed
  • Remove nail polish and jewelry
  • Arrange for transportation home after the procedure

After the procedure

Following your dialysis catheter placement:

  • Keep the catheter site clean and dry between treatments
  • Protect the catheter from bumps and contamination
  • Use the catheter as directed during dialysis sessions
  • Watch for signs of infection (redness, warmth, discharge)
  • Avoid strenuous arm activity on the catheter side
  • Attend all scheduled dialysis appointments
