Meeting the Specifications

My site meets all specifications set out for this assignment. The site uses common headers and footers in conjunction with the HTML include script. This allows the header to be identical between all pages. The header contains the name of the site, as well as a navigation bar, which allows users to navigate to any other page within the site. The other pages this site contains are the about page, which provides users with a background of the site, as well as why they might find the site useful. The procedure search page allows users to search for their procedure, and once a user selects their procedure, they are brought to a dedicated page about that procedure. The support page allows users to reach out with any questions that may not have been answered within the procedure page.

Dynamic functionality

This site utilizes a variety of dynmaic features that ideally make the site easier and more intuitive for a wider variety of users to interact with. The first of these features is the procedure search, which utilizes Javascript to search through all of the procedures on the site based on the user's search input. The second dynamic feature is the accordion effect, which can be found on each procedure page. This allows users to collapse different sections of the procedure page, which helps users avoid feeling overwhelmed by the amount of information on each procedure page. Finally, this site uses a Formspree API to allow users to directly reach out with questions. This form allows users to input a question, and once they submit the form, the question is automatically sent out by email.

Feedback based Improvements

I received some great feedback from the peer review assignment from a few weeks ago. One of the main points that was raised was the lack of visual elements throughout the site. To address this, I added a variety of open source images that relate to the overall content of the site. Another point that was raised was the somewhat limited color scheme throughout the site. While I do believe that having an interesting color palette is important, in the end I decided to maintain my original color scheme. This choice was mainly influenced by the purpose of the site. While I do want the site to be interesting, the overall purpose of the site is to provide users with information about radiological medical procedures. I feel that by having a simple color scheme, users are more likely to focus on the content of the site itself.

How well does it work? | Future plans

There are a variety of websites that provide patients with information about medical procedures, so my site is in no way reinventing the wheel. However, my site is different from these other sites in its source of information. All of the information provided on my site is guaranteed to have been thoroughly verified and verified by a radiologist. This allows users to feel confident that the information they are receiving is accurate. Additionally, I think that radiologists could also benefit from my site, as it has the potential to serve as a hub for quickly briefing patients on their procedure before it happens. In the future, I would like to expand the number of procedures contained on the site, as currently there are only ten. Furthermore, I would like to consider how I could focus and improve the design of my site to cater more towards a briefing-style, where radiologists could quickly and easily find a given procedure, and allow their patients to review the procedure themselves.
